<h1>About Me</h1>
<p class="lead">There is more to my life than research, teaching, and academic conferences.</p>
<p>I live in Eau Claire, Wisconsin, with my wife and daughter, and we love calling this place home. Outside of work, I enjoy spending time with my family, traveling, hiking, and being outdoors. Mountains and beautiful landscapes are especially appealing to me.</p>
<h2>Beyond Academia</h2>
<p>One of my favorite creative outlets is nature and landscape photography. I enjoy the entire process, not just taking the picture: finding a scene, capturing it, editing the image, and eventually printing it. Some of my photographs become part of our home decor, while others make their way onto <a href="https://www.instagram.com/srbtm_b" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">my Instagram</a>.</p>
<p>It's a hobby that combines my love of travel and the outdoors with the satisfaction of creating something I can enjoy and share long after a trip is over.</p>
